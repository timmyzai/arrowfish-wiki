Arrowfish VPN — Business PRD

适用对象：BA · PM · 非技术团队成员 For: BA · PM · Non-technical team members 用途：产品上手参考 · PRD 素材 Use: Product orientation · PRD source

一、产品是什么1. What Is The Product

Arrowfish VPN 是一款面向中文用户的商业 VPN 服务，帮助用户安全访问 Google、YouTube、社交媒体等被中国防火长城屏蔽的境外网站。

Arrowfish VPN is a commercial VPN service for Chinese-speaking users, giving them safe access to Google, YouTube, social media, and other overseas sites blocked by China's Great Firewall.

全球 156 个 VPN 节点，覆盖多国。连接建立超过 4 秒视为失败；手机锁屏超过 5 分钟无流量，服务端自动断连；修改账号权限后节点最多需要 60 秒同步。

156 global VPN nodes across many countries. A connection that takes more than 4 seconds to establish counts as a failure; if the phone is locked with no traffic for more than 5 minutes the server auto-disconnects; account-permission changes take up to 60 seconds to sync to the nodes.

⚠️防火长城封锁风险：中国大陆 GFW 会封锁 VPN 节点 IP、协议流量特征，乃至登录域名。封锁可无预警触发，且省级策略不同。这是节点不稳定投诉的根本原因，与节点服务商质量无关。
⚠️GFW blocking risk: China's Great Firewall blocks VPN node IPs, protocol traffic signatures, and even login domains. Blocks can fire without warning, and provincial policies differ. This is the root cause of node-instability complaints — unrelated to provider quality.

十一、营销与活动11. Marketing & Campaigns

系统追踪用户注册来源渠道（Google广告、百度、视频推广等），可按渠道统计注册量与转化率。活动支持限时折扣、注册送券、购买赠时等形式，由管理员配置有效期和发放条件。

The system tracks the channel each user came from (Google Ads, Baidu, video promos, etc.), so registrations and conversion rates can be sliced per channel. Campaigns support limited-time discounts, sign-up coupons, purchase bonuses, and similar formats — admins configure validity periods and eligibility.
